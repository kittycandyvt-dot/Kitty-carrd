THE STORY OF KITTYCANDYVT

From lab cat to internet celebrity.

Kitty was a scientist's cat who spent her days around the laboratory, watching experiments and getting into places she probably shouldn't have.

One fateful day, Kitty jumped into a vat of chemically induced cotton candy. The experiment changed her forever — transforming her into a human-cat hybrid.

But becoming a catgirl wasn't the only tragedy that day.

Her scientist guardian later passed away in a devastating laboratory fire, leaving Kitty on her own.

Now she lives on as a cat hybrid, trying to disguise her true nature by becoming a VTuber and voice actress on the internet. Surely nobody will notice the ears, tail, suspiciously cat-like behavior, or the fact that she may have been created in a laboratory... right?
